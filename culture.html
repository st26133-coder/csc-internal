Food & Culture

Learn about the ingredients and traditions behind Sri Lankan food.

🌶️ Spices

Spices are an important part of Sri Lankan cooking. Common spices include cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, chilli, turmeric and curry leaves.

🥥 Coconut

Coconut is used in many Sri Lankan dishes. Coconut milk is often used to make curries, while grated coconut can be used in sambols and desserts.

🍚 Rice

Rice is an important staple food in Sri Lanka and is commonly served with a variety of curries and side dishes.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family & Food

Food is an important part of Sri Lankan family life. Meals are often shared with family and friends, especially during celebrations and special occasions.
